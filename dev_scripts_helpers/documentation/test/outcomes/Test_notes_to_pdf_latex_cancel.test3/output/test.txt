\section{Treatment Effect
Heterogeneity}\label{treatment-effect-heterogeneity}

Individual treatment effects depend on both observed and unobserved
outcomes:

\[
\tau_i = Y_i(1) - Y_i(0)
\]

We observe: - \(Y_i(1)\) when \(T_i = 1\) - \(\cancel{Y_i(0)}\) when
\(T_i = 1\) (unobserved) - \(\cancel{Y_i(1)}\) when \(T_i = 0\)
(unobserved) - \(Y_i(0)\) when \(T_i = 0\)

Hence \(\tau_i\) is not directly identifiable without assumptions.
