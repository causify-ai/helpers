# Send Emails to External People

## Introduction

- This guide covers emails to people outside the team
  - E.g., investors, professors, companies, external collaborators
- An external email is visible to people who do not know our context
  - A mistake is hard to undo
  - Therefore the emails follow a review process before they are sent
- For internal communication see
  [`all.team_collaboration.how_to_guide.md`](/docs/work_organization/all.team_collaboration.how_to_guide.md#use-the-right-form-of-communication)

## Get an Email Reviewed Before Sending It

- Use this protocol for an important email, e.g., to an investor, a professor, a
  company, or an external collaborator
  - Do not send it before it is reviewed
- Choose one of two paths, based on how sure you are about the draft

### Path A: You Are About 99% Sure the Draft Is Ok

- Email the draft to the reviewer (e.g., GP)
- Start the subject with `DRAFT:`, followed by the subject of the proposed email
- Write the proposed email in full in the body, with To, Cc, Subject, and text
- The reviewer only needs to reply "ack"
  - After the ack, send the email as it is
- If the reviewer wants changes, apply them and send the email

### Path B: The Draft Needs Edits or Advice

- Put the draft in the `Email` Gdoc of the project
  - The link is in the Resources list of `docs/08-how_we_work.md` of the project
- Ask the team to edit the draft and to add comments in the Gdoc
  - Use suggestion mode, so that everybody can see who changed what
- Update the draft from the edits and the comments
- When the draft is ready, send it, or follow Path A to get an ack
- Do not paste the same draft in both places, see [DRY also applies to
  documentation](/docs/work_organization/all.team_collaboration.how_to_guide.md#dry-also-applies-to-documentation)

## Discuss an External Email Thread Internally

- Never discuss a thread with external people on the same thread
  - A reply to the external thread can reach the external people by mistake
- To start an "internal" thread from an external thread:
  - Reply to, or forward, the external email to the internal people
  - Add spaces at the end of the subject
    - The subject looks the same to humans
    - The internal discussion is a separate thread from the external one
  - Remove all the external people from To, Cc, and Bcc
    - Check each address, not only the names
    - Check the domain of each address, since a name can hide an external address
  - Read the recipients list one more time before sending
- Keep the internal thread and the external thread separate
  - Do not reply all on the internal thread to the external people
  - When the team agrees on the answer, write the reply on the external thread and
    follow the review process in "Get an Email Reviewed Before Sending It"
